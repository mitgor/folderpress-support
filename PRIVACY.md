FolderPress Privacy Policy
Publisher: Woodenshark LLC
Effective date: 18 October 2026

FolderPress processes files locally on your Mac. It does not collect, upload, sell, or share your files or personal information. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, user accounts, or network capability.

You choose the files and folders FolderPress may access through macOS. The app reads file contents and metadata to analyze storage, create compressed or decompressed replacement files, and verify their integrity. These operations are local. Paths, results, and byte counts are kept in memory for the current session. macOS may store standard application settings such as window position and file-picker history.

You may explicitly export a local report containing selected file paths, operation outcomes, error messages, and storage figures. You control where you save that report and whether you share it. macOS may retain standard diagnostic information according to your system settings.

The app does not retain access bookmarks to your selected files between launches. Removing the app does not reverse compression already applied to your files; macOS continues to read transparently compressed files. You can use FolderPress's Decompress action before uninstalling if desired.

Support: support@woodenshark.com. If you contact support, Woodenshark LLC receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond to your request. Public GitHub issues are publicly visible; never include private file contents or personal paths. GitHub and your email provider process information according to their own policies.
